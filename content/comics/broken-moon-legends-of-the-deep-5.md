Korbin, David, Avery, Keeney, and To’klace, along with their reluctant vampire allies, set out by boat to find the only creature who might be able to stop Cthulhu and the Tok-tok: Dagon. But Dagon is guarded by sea serpents and not even guaranteed to awaken, and Cthulhu is spreading a madness akin to nuclear radiation. The evocative horror art of Nat Jones brings the latest chapter of AGP’s post-apocalyptic hit to thrilling life! Note: The digital edition (6/7/2017) for this issue was released before the print edition.
