Sir Gottfrey arrives aboard Domus Mariae searching for Double-M. Father Micon takes the bullets intended for Elano and dies in his disciple’s arms. Once inside the replika ghetto, Elano stumbles into the crossfire between two rival gangs and ends up captured by Klaudette.
